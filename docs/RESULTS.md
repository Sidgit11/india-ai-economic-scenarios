## Sensitivity of the India results (substantial · extreme)

Base India calibration first; each row then changes one Indian input.

| Variant | GDP % above no-AI | Cognitive wage % | Cognitive unemp. % | All unemp. % | Labor share % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base India | 3.0 · 9.3 | -5.0 · -27.8 | 9.6 · 26.2 | 4.5 · 7.6 | 51.7 · 48.3 |
| wage-bill weighted cognitive share (0.35) | 4.5 · 14.7 | -3.4 · -23.2 | 8.1 · 24.4 | 4.7 · 9.6 | 51.0 · 45.8 |
| inelastic capital (eps=1) | 2.3 · 6.5 | -6.4 · -32.1 | 9.5 · 25.9 | 4.4 · 7.5 | 51.3 · 47.1 |
| perfectly elastic capital (eps=inf) | 3.7 · 12.0 | -3.5 · -23.2 | 9.6 · 26.6 | 4.5 · 7.6 | 52.0 · 49.5 |
| rigid formal wages (xi=0.9) | 2.6 · 8.0 | -0.7 · -4.9 | 11.2 · 34.5 | 4.9 · 9.2 | 51.7 · 48.4 |
| flexible wages (xi=0) | 3.8 · 10.7 | -15.3 · -62.0 | 6.0 · 5.7 | 3.5 · 3.2 | 51.6 · 48.2 |
| slower absorption (f=0.10/month) | 3.1 · 9.3 | -5.1 · -28.0 | 9.7 · 26.4 | 4.3 · 7.5 | 51.7 · 48.3 |
| adoption lag: 2030 diffusion halved | 1.6 · 5.4 | -2.5 · -11.9 | 7.8 · 14.2 | 4.3 · 5.2 | 52.3 · 50.6 |

Reading the table: "wage-bill weighted cognitive share" uses 35% (cognitive workers' share of pay) instead of 23% (share of heads). "adoption lag" halves the 2030 diffusion share. eps is the capital supply elasticity, xi the yearly rigidity of the cognitive wage.
